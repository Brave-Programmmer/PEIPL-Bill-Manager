## Design Context

### Users
A billing operator uses PEIPL BILL on a Windows desktop while scanning invoice folders, reviewing bill status, editing invoices, and preparing GeM uploads. The job is repetitive, detail-sensitive, and benefits from quick guidance without leaving the workflow.

### Brand Personality
Energetic, practical, personal. The companion should feel like a helpful teammate: concise, upbeat, and never distracting from billing work.

### Aesthetic Direction
Keep the existing light/dark glass-and-blue application language, with a distinct warm amber/coral companion accent. The companion is a compact utility surface, not a marketing chatbot or a large modal. Support both light and dark themes and reduced motion.

### Design Principles
- Lead with useful next actions and dashboard-aware guidance.
- Keep local, privacy-preserving behavior available offline.
- Make optional online AI explicit and user-controlled.
- Use compact, readable conversation UI with strong focus states.
- Prefer direct navigation and actions over long explanations.
